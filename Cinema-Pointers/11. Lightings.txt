LIGHT TYPES -
	Natural - Sunlight, Moonlight, Firelight, Candlelight, Thunder
	Artificial - Manmade using electricity
		
	Ambient - Lights sources on location (Street lamps)
	Practical - Light sources in frame
	Motivated - Source that is shown as the cause of light (Candle)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Contrast - Difference b/w brightest point and darkest point

HIGH KEY LIGHT - Low  Contrast - Difference b/w light and dark is low (Happy)
LOW KEY LIGHT  - High Contrast - Difference b/w light and dark is high (Intense)

HARD LIGHT - Staight sharp light. Creates stronger shadows
SOFT LIGHT - Diffused light. Creates spreaded softer shadows

DUMB SIDE - Light falls on the side of the subject facing the camera
SMART SIDE - Light falls on the side of the subject not facing the camera

BOKEH - Out of focus points of light

	Tungsten - Warm light
	HMI - Cool/ Day light
	Fluorescent - Tube light
	Kino Flo - Collection of tubes
	LED - RGB/ colors

LIGHT EQUIPMENTS -
	Gels - Sheets to add color to light
	Diffusors - To Scatter and Soften light
		Soft box, Diff paper, Silk cloth, Fog, Dust, Smoke, Cloud
	Bounce - Diffuse and redirect light
		Reflector, Bead board
	Flags - To block the light
		Black screens or boards, Barn doors on light
	Egg crates - To direct light and prevent light spillage in unwanted areas
	Cookies - Boards with pattern to add texture (Window shape)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LIGHT TYPES by POSITION -
	Key light - Primary light on the subject
	Fill light - Fill the shadows caused by key
	Back light - Creates edge to separate subject from background
	Edge lights/ Kickers - Edge of a particular area like ears
	Hair lights - Edges of the hair
	Catch light - Reflection in the eyes to add life
	Background lights - Lights that are contrasty to the foreground

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LIGHTING TECHNIQUES -
	Rembrandt - Key light higher, Fill light dimmer 
		Creates a triangle on neg side. Creates intense
	Butterfly/ Paramount - Key at high infront
		Butterfly shape under the nose. Shows Glamour
	Loop - Key light at 45°
		Elongates the face
	Split - Key light at eye, No Fill light
		Adds contrast and mystery
	Overhead/ Top - Over head of subject hides eyes
		Helpless or Lifeless
	Underlighting - Under the face
		Adds horror and scare
	For Dark Lighting -
		Use Ceiling Bounce technique with a 6500K

Light Meter -
Gives the light measurement.

Incident Light - How much light is falling from the source on the subject.
Reflective Light  - How much light is falling from the subject to the camera.

White - 100% Reflective
Middle Gray - 18% Reflective (Halfway between white and black that human eye can see)
Black - 0% Reflective

To use the light meter set the ISO, Shutter Speed and Frame rate in the meter to match the camera.
Aperture set in the camera is considered as Middle Gray for that shot.

Light meter gives the values in F stops -
F1, F1.4, F2.8, F4, F5.6, F8, F11, F18, F22, F32
With every stop the light's intensity doubles or halves.

Assume camera aperture is F2.8.
F2.8 is now considered as the Middle gray.
This means if reflective light value is F2.8 then that is 18% Gray.

For brightness, the light must be set to get F more than Middle Gray i.e. more than 2.8.
For darkness, the light must be set to get F less than Middle Gray i.e. less than 2.8.

For a good shot - T
The smart/ short side must have F more than middle gray (F5.6 or F8).
The dumb/ long short side must have F more than short side (F5.6 or F8).
Skin tones must be 1 or 1.5 stops above Middle Gray (F4).

The difference between short side and long side is measured in rations.
More the ratio more dramatic the shot gets.
If long side has F1.4 and short side has F8 which has a difference of 4 Stops then ratio is (2^4) 16:1.
